03 · Deployment

Cloud, On-Premise, or Hybrid —
choose based on your data, workload, and business.

Deployment should fit your data, workload, and business. AI Center helps you pick the right model.

CLOUD

Cloud Deployment

Fast launch · Elastic scaling
Best for:
  • Rapid POC, elastic scaling, low upfront cost
  • Short-cycle experiments, but watch data governance
ON-PREM

On-Premise

Data sovereignty · Low latency
Best for:
  • Data security, dedicated performance, predictable cost
  • Data stays in-house, but requires capacity planning
HYBRID

Hybrid

Flexible architecture · Balanced governance
Best for:
  • Enterprise AI architecture balancing governance & agility
  • Combines cloud elasticity with on-prem control
